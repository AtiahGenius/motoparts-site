Privacy notice

This prototype stores demo account, inventory, reservation, and preference data in your browser's local storage.

What is stored

Account details, saved shops, reservations, contact activity, reviews, and basic search interactions may be stored locally to make the prototype work between pages.

What is not stored

Motify does not process online payments in this phase. Payment details are not requested or stored by the platform.

External services

Maps, image hosting, and WhatsApp links may connect to third-party services when you choose to use them. Their own privacy terms apply.
