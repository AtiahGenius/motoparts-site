Using the platform

Terms of use

Motify helps riders discover local shops. It does not sell parts directly or guarantee vendor stock.

Reservations

A reservation is a request to hold a part. The vendor confirms availability and payment is made directly to the vendor on pickup.

Listings and reviews

Vendors are responsible for accurate listings. Riders should verify fitment, price, and availability with the shop before travelling.

Respectful use

Use contact details, maps, and review tools responsibly. Do not submit misleading reviews or misuse another person's account.
