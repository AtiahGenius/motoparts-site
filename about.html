About the platform

Bridging riders, vendors, and fitters in Kwabenya

Motify solves a specific, local problem: riders in Kwabenya waste time and fuel searching shop to shop for spare parts, without knowing which vendors have stock, which shops are open, or how to reach a trusted fitter.

What we solve

Component misidentification, unverified vendor locations, uncertain stock, and no quick way to reach installation help. Every listing on the platform carries the shop's exact landmark, live open/closed status, and the assigned mechanic's direct contact - bound to the product, not buried in a profile page.

Phase 1: no online payment

Every reservation is a hold, not a transaction. Riders pay the vendor directly in cash or mobile money when they arrive to collect the part. This keeps the platform simple, fast to adopt, and free of payment-gateway friction for a peri-urban market that runs on cash and MoMo.

What's next

Phase 2 introduces MTN MoMo and Telecel Cash payment integration, delivery-rider dispatch for parts fulfillment, and in-app mechanic booking.
